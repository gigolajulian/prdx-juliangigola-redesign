# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user choice). Multi-page: Home, About, Locations, Contact — mirroring the current prdx.us structure.

## Users

Bay Area clients looking for a barber (and returning clients finding their shop); aspiring barbers considering the ĒDUCŌ licensing program; fans of the /Paradox/ brand and its clothing.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for /Paradox/, a contemporary barber collective founded in San Jose in 2015. Primary job: communicate the brand, its story and culture. Booking a cut is the secondary (but always-present) action; Academy and Shop are supporting paths.

## Positioning

A brotherhood of barbers forged in barber college in 2011, five Bay Area shops, and its own licensed barber academy (ĒDUCŌ) housed in the Downtown San Jose HQ. "Purveyors of style." "A hundred years is the horizon."

## Brand Commitments

- Name written with slashes: /Paradox/, /P/, /MISSION/ etc. ĒDUCŌ with macrons.
- Existing white serif wordmark (img/logo.webp) and black paisley bandana pattern (img/paisley.webp).
- Copy reused from the current site; do not invent claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Photos in img/: shop interiors per location, four in-shop barber portraits, an editorial nape/haircut shot. No testimonials, pricing, or hours exist — do not fabricate them. Contact page has no email/phone on the current site.

## Product Principles

1. Brand and culture lead; the booking path is never more than one tap away.
2. Precision is the brand — every detail should feel cut, not approximated.
3. Use their real people and spaces; no stock or invented content.
