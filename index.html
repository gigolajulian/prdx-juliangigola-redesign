Become a licensed barber /ĒDUCŌ/

An academy by /Paradox/
Downtown San Jose

ĒDUCŌ operates on two planes; offering a barber licensing program built to train aspiring barbers on the technical skills to become a certified licensed professional within the state of California.

While simultaneously serving as an education platform sharing our proven philosophy that’s grown and strengthened the foundation of the /Paradox/ collective.

The academy serves as a communal space for our global network of creators to not only inspire students, but empower them to create with the intentionality and build from within as we aim to push the industry forward though a perspective that’s uniquely our own.

  1. I.

    CA License

    Become an official CA licensed barber.

  2. II.

    Modern Teaching

    Learn the latest and greatest techniques in barbering and styling with educators from around the world.

  3. III.

    Official /P/ Barber

    After the academy you will have a shot to become an official /Paradox/ barber.
