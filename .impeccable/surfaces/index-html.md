# Surface: prdx.us redesign (Home, About, Locations, Contact)

Mode: Persuade. Brand first, booking second (always one tap away). Static multi-page HTML/CSS/JS.
Audience: Bay Area clients, aspiring barbers (ĒDUCŌ), brand followers. Proof: real shop and barber photos, real history and addresses. No invented hours/prices/testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: /Paradox/ published as a street-culture magazine issue. Every page is a feature spread with folios, feature numbers and captions doing real navigation. Refuses the dark-luxury barbershop hero with gold serif and a centered Book button.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper white #F2F2EE and ink #0E0E0E, one spot ink: Barbicide blue (#00A3C4 range) used as solid fields and stickers, never as glow. Paisley bandana as endpaper. Condensed grotesk display at extreme scale against small mono folios/captions; the slash is the only ornament. Photos print as ink halftone/grayscale and develop to colour on entry. Hairline rules, numbered features, cover lines.

STORY: Visitor sees a confident cover and understands: contemporary barber collective, San Jose, since 2015, five shops, their own academy, their own clothes. They believe it through real people and spaces, and book or go deeper.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Magazine cover. Masthead /Paradox/ spans full width at top (wordmark scale). Nape photo as cover image right/center, bleeding. Left column: numbered cover lines 01 Cuts / 02 ĒDUCŌ Academy / 03 Shop / 04 Five Shops, each a link. Folio strip: "Est. 2015 — San Jose, CA", "Vol. 11". Spot-blue round sticker "Book an appointment" overlapping the photo edge. Nav as a thin contents strip.

FORM: Japanese street-culture magazine, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list. Seed key 4502e329. Raises: two inks + spot with photo develop-on-entry (cabaret); vertical folio rail with 2011 → 2126 year axis (deep dive); designed absence in shop index states (seven-segment). Signature interaction: halftone-to-colour photo develop + scroll-driven folio/year rail.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
